AuthorOps-Pipeline

Composable workflows for reliable AI-assisted production of manuscripts, papers, books, research, and serious knowledge work.

"Skills provide capabilities. Pipelines provide execution patterns."

Pipeline Registry

  • document-review — Review document for structure, style, and evidence.
  • academic-paper — Paper production with evidence, citation, and verification gates.
  • manuscript-development — Iterative authoring and stage-by-stage revision.
  • literature-review — Systematic literature search, extraction, and synthesis.
  • submission-readiness — Publishing venue compliance and final checks.

Architecture & Principles

  • Artifact-first: Transforms defined artifacts.
  • Explicit stages: Inspectable execution order and inputs/outputs.
  • Quality before completion: Mandatory quality gates.
  • Human-controllable: First-class review & approval points.
